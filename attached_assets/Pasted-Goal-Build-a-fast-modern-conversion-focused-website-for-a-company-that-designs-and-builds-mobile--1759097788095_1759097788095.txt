Goal: Build a fast, modern, conversion-focused website for a company that designs and builds mobile tyre vans. Outperform the reference site in clarity, UX, lead capture, and SEO. Replace external Jotform flows with a native, step-by-step configurator and proper stock CMS.

Tech stack (Replit-friendly):
	•	Frontend: React + Vite + TypeScript + TailwindCSS + shadcn/ui
	•	Router: React Router
	•	Backend: Node.js + Express (TypeScript)
	•	DB: SQLite (dev) and Postgres-ready (prod) via Drizzle ORM
	•	Email: Nodemailer (SMTP creds via env) + simple queue
	•	Forms security: hCaptcha/ReCAPTCHA + rate limiting (express-rate-limit)
	•	Images: responsive <img srcset> + sharp for server-side optimization
	•	SEO: meta tags, Open Graph, Twitter cards, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, schema.org JSON-LD

Brand & UI:
	•	Clean, bold, industrial feel. Primary: #111827, accents #FACC15 and #2563EB. Rounded-2xl cards, soft shadows, big typography, plenty of white space. Sticky top nav with CTA button.

Core pages & routes:
	1.	/ Home
	•	Above-the-fold hero: headline, subheadline, primary CTA “Configure Your Van”, secondary “Browse Stock”.
	•	4-step process cards: Choose Van → Choose Kit → Upgrades → Funding.
	•	Trust strip: badges (FCA authorised text placeholder), “Nationwide Delivery”, “UK-built”.
	•	Featured stock carousel (3–6 items).
	•	Customer logos + short testimonials.
	•	CTA band and mini FAQ.
	2.	/stock Stock listing
	•	Filters: Make, Year, Mileage, Price range, LWB/SWB, Transmission.
	•	Grid of cards with image, key specs, price + VAT, “View” + “Start Configurator”.
	3.	/stock/:slug Vehicle detail
	•	Gallery (lightbox), spec table, finance example widget (non-advisory disclaimer), “Start Configurator with this Van”, enquiry form.
	4.	/configurator Multi-step (client-side state + autosave)
	•	Step 1: Base Van (or “I’ll choose stock later”).
	•	Step 2: Tyre Kit (balancer, changer, compressor; show power draw, footprint).
	•	Step 3: Upgrades (wrap, lighting, CCTV, racking, inverter, branding, security).
	•	Step 4: Summary + lead capture (name, email, phone, company) + “Request Quote”.
	•	On submit: create quotation record + email PDF summary to user and sales inbox.
	5.	/finance Finance info & enquiry
	•	Static explainer, eligibility checklist, enquiry form (no advice given; FCA copy placeholder).
	6.	/gallery Before/after builds, reels embed.
	7.	/about Company story, facility photos, team.
	8.	/contact Map, form, phone, email, hours.
	9.	/legal/{privacy,cookies,terms}

CMS (simple admin): /admin (basic auth)
	•	Vans CRUD (title, slug, make, model, year, mileage, price, VAT flag, images, heroImage, specs JSON).
	•	Kits CRUD (name, description, inclusions, power requirements).
	•	Upgrades CRUD (name, category, price, notes).
	•	Leads & Quotes view/export (CSV).
	•	Media uploader (stores to /uploads).

Data models (Drizzle):
	•	vans: id, slug, title, make, model, year (int), mileage (int), price (int, pence), vatIncluded (bool), specs (json), images (json[]), createdAt, updatedAt, published (bool)
	•	kits: id, name, description, includes (json[]), powerKw (decimal), price (int, pence), createdAt, updatedAt, published
	•	upgrades: id, name, category, description, price (int, pence), createdAt, updatedAt, published
	•	quotes: id, userName, email, phone, company, vanId (nullable), kitId, selectedUpgradeIds (json[]), notes, estSubtotal (int), estVAT (int), estTotal (int), createdAt
	•	leads: id, name, email, phone, source, message, createdAt

Configurator logic:
	•	Real-time price summary (subtotal + VAT toggle).
	•	Persist selections to localStorage; also create server draft on step change.
	•	Generate a branded PDF quote (company logo, line items, totals, legal footer).
	•	Email both customer and sales inbox; store quoteId for follow-up.

Components to implement:
