⚠️ IMPORTANT ADDITION to the SKU & BOM system.

Looking at the upgrades, many parent upgrades have 
VARIANTS (child upgrades). Each variant is a 
completely separate product with its own SKU and 
its own Bill of Materials.

For example:
Parent: "Accessories" (no SKU — just a group header)
  ├── Variant: Pack 1 - Maxi Euro 8 with T5000 & Mini Spin
  │     SKU: PKG-MAXI-T5000-MINI
  │     BOM: T5000 Pro Tyre Machine x1
  │          Mini Spin Balancer x1
  │          Maxi Euro 8 Lift x1
  │
  ├── Variant: Pack 2 - Euro 8 with T5000 & Mini Spin  
  │     SKU: PKG-EURO8-T5000-MINI
  │     BOM: T5000 Pro Tyre Machine x1
  │          Mini Spin Balancer x1
  │          Euro 8 Lift x1
  │
  └── Variant: Pack 3 etc...
        SKU: PKG-MINI-T5000-MINI
        BOM: different components...

The rules are:

1. PARENT upgrades that have variants:
   - Do NOT need a SKU (they are just group headers)
   - Do NOT need a BOM
   - In the SKU manager show them greyed out 
     with label "Group — see variants"

2. VARIANT upgrades (children):
   - MUST have their own individual SKU
   - MUST have their own individual BOM
   - Each variant's BOM can be completely different
   - In the SKU manager show them indented under 
     their parent with full SKU + BOM editing

3. SIMPLE upgrades (no variants):
   - Have their own SKU
   - Have their own BOM (or just their own SKU 
     if they are a single component)

4. When the "Push to AutoTradeOS" button is clicked 
   on a build sheet:
   - If the selected item is a VARIANT:
     Push THAT variant's SKU and BOM components
   - If the selected item is a SIMPLE upgrade:
     Push that upgrade's SKU and BOM
   - If the selected item is a PARENT GROUP:
     This should never happen — customer always 
     selects a specific variant, never the parent

5. In the SKU Manager admin page:
   Display like this:

   ACCESSORIES (group — no SKU needed)
     ├── Pack 1 Maxi Euro 8 T5000  [SKU: PKG-MAXI-T5000] [Edit BOM]
     ├── Pack 2 Euro 8 T5000       [SKU: PKG-EURO8-T5000] [Edit BOM]
     ├── Pack 3 Mini Euro 8        [SKU: ⚠️ No SKU] [Add SKU]
     └── Pack 4 Maxi Euro 8 T6000  [SKU: ⚠️ No SKU] [Add SKU]

   3000W INVERTER (simple — own SKU)
     [SKU: INV-3000W] [Edit BOM]

   48V SYSTEM (simple — own SKU, complex BOM)
     [SKU: SYS-48V] [Edit BOM]
     BOM: INV-48V x1, BAT-48V x2, BMS x1...

6. The pre-flight warning before pushing to 
   AutoTradeOS should check:
   - Any selected VARIANT with no SKU → warn
   - Any selected SIMPLE upgrade with no SKU → warn
   - Parent groups → skip (not applicable)
   - Show count: "3 of 12 items have no SKU"
